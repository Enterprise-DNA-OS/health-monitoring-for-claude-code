# Why this rebuild

Chosen 20 October 2026 as the first rebuild in the occupational health family.

- **The bill.** Cority publishes no price. Its pricing page says what an organisation pays depends on the solutions it uses, the number and type of users and the integrations it needs, with the subscription committed over an initial three-year term and an annual uplift, plus a one-time implementation project for the consultants who configure it (https://www.cority.com/pricing/, checked 20 October 2026). Occupational Health is one of its Health solutions.
- **Data-shaped.** Employers, workers, exposure groups, tests and results, reports, removals, actions, drug and alcohol tests, invoices, and weekly rituals: the recall list, chasing reports, giving workers their copy, telling the regulator.
- **The rules are real and checkable.** The NZ GRWM Regulations 2016 regs 31 to 42 and the Australian WHS Regulations rr 58, 368 to 378 and 405 to 418 turn into dates and refusals: when a test is due, who must get the report, when a worker must come out of lead work and when they can go back.
- **What stays with the custom build.** Device links to audiometers and spirometers, a worker portal and a collector app.
